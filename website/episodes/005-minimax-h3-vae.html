EPISODE 005 / LOCAL VIDEO GENERATION

A faster step.
Not twice the whole speed.

Episode 005 · 45-second episode · Published

45 seconds · Captions included

Watch on YouTube ↗ · Watch on Instagram ↗

ComfyUI reports faster encoding and decoding for MiniMax H3’s video VAE—the component that compresses frames for the model and reconstructs them afterwards. The improvement concerns this stage of the workflow. [1]

What did the test show?

In the developer’s RTX 5090 test, a 1344 × 768 video with 129 frames took 24.3 seconds before and 12.7 seconds after the update for an encode/decode round trip. These are developer measurements, not our own benchmark. [1]

Why not twice as fast overall?

Encoding and decoding are only part of making a video. Faster VAE processing does not establish the same improvement for a complete render.

Does the picture change?

The developer says the quality difference is unlikely to be visible by eye. That is an attributed assessment, not our independent finding. [1]

What it means for Rogue Signal

We use MiniMax H3 and ComfyUI to make Rogue. Claude’s production notes record ComfyUI 0.37.4, an fp16 video VAE and no fast fp16-accumulation setting. Some improvements may already apply; we have not run a controlled before-and-after test.

Our next useful measurement would separate time spent in each stage while comparing the resulting footage. We are not claiming a speed gain for our RTX 5080 setup.
